# Background: LionMatch

LionMatch is a web app made by five Columbia first-years (Alex, Jun, Max, Peter and Ayan). It placed 3rd at the hackathon on November 21, 2026. Live at lionmatch.vercel.app.

What it does today:
- A Tinder-style swipe deck of Columbia internships, clubs, research groups, fellowships and people. Right is Interested, left is Pass, up is Save.
- Every card shows a match score out of 10 and the reasons behind it (shared skills, interests, major, what you are looking for).
- The deck learns from swipes and re-ranks the cards behind the top one.
- Explore: search and sort everything, hide closed listings, show only listings whose course prerequisites you meet.
- Listing details: location, time commitment, deadline, majors, skills, prerequisites, official link, sometimes a person to talk to.
- People profiles with coffee chat and outreach messages drafted for you, which land in Chats.
- Preferences, plus import from a PDF resume or LinkedIn export (read in the browser, never uploaded).
- History of swipes and what the deck learned.

What is in it: 48 listings (18 clubs, 15 research groups, 9 internships, 6 fellowships) and 12 people profiles. Data is entered by hand from official pages.

What it does not have yet (ideas, none decided): deadline reminders, a mobile app, real accounts and sign-in, club fair mode, real messaging with other students, listings added by clubs and labs themselves, alumni and upperclassmen mentors, application tracking.

The team signs surveys as "the LionMatch team".
